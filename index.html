How to use

Simple daily routine

1

Set clean date

Enter the day the life changing decision was made. This powers the clean and serene counter and monthly trophies.

2

Set weekly goal

Choose how many meetings to aim for each week. Online meetings can count if needed.

3

Plan meetings

Use the NA and AA finders, then add chosen meetings to the weekly plan.

4

Check in

After a meeting, tap in person or online. The app updates progress, badges, trophies and journal history.

5

Use crisis mode

If struggling, go straight to the support section for online meetings and helpline options.

6

Keep it simple

The only real aim is one day at a time. Use the app as a support tool, not as pressure.
